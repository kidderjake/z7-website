# ZoneSeven design review

Source: https://zone7.au/ (reviewed 18 September 2026).

## Assessment
The original oversized wordmark is memorable and the three-service structure is easy to understand. However, the particle orb competes with the typography, faint metadata reduces readability, and a mix of three typefaces makes the hierarchy less disciplined. Identical rounded service cards underplay the differences between the offerings. The numerical counters communicate positioning rather than evidence, so their animated treatment overstates their importance. The custom cursor and continuous animations add complexity without helping the enquiry journey. The email field and button are not connected to a form or submission action.

## Applied direction
A restrained, architectural graphic language for a business owner comparing digital partners on a laptop in a bright office. Cool paper, navy ink and pale blue create clear contrast. Archivo was selected for the direct, engineered, purposeful voice of its sans-serif shapes. A striped seven replaces the generic orb with a motif tied to the name. Clear service rows use thin rules, generous space and three small CSS diagrams. A dark approach section changes the pace without adding visual clutter. The original Automate, Amplify, Accelerate and Enter the Zone language is retained.

## Usability
Responsive stacked layouts, mobile navigation, labelled fields, native email validation, keyboard focus, skip navigation and reduced-motion support. Service links preselect their subject in the enquiry form. No case studies, customer logos or outcome statistics have been invented.

## Enquiry delivery
The preview copies the completed project brief, with a downloadable fallback if clipboard access is unavailable. It explicitly says that the brief has not been sent. Connect the form only after the receiving email or submission service is confirmed. The original website is linked separately. The original live domain has not been changed.
